# 🍎 nystreets - Bring the Bustle of the Big Apple to Your World! 🍎

Is your Minetest world feeling a little empty? Do you long for the energetic hustle and bustle of a thriving metropolis? Look no further! **nystreets** breathes life into your server by introducing vibrant, walking NPCs that transform quiet roads into the lively streets of New York City! 🗽🚕

## ✨ Features That Will Transform Your Game ✨

- 🚶‍♂️ **Dynamic Global Spawner**: Watch your city come alive! Our intelligent spawning system dynamically populates the area around active players with a bustling crowd, seamlessly managing the population up to a configurable limit to keep the action perfectly balanced.
- 👗 **Massive Wardrobe (132 Unique Skins!)**: Every NPC is a unique individual! With an incredible 132 different character skins randomly assigned, your streets will boast unprecedented diversity.
- 🧹 **`clear_multitud` Chat Command**: Complete control at your fingertips. Need to clear the streets in an instant? Server admins can quickly and cleanly remove all `nystreets:npc` entities from the world with a single command. Perfect for events or resets!
- 🥚 **NPC Spawn Egg (`nystreets:egg`)**: Be the ultimate city planner! Manually place NPCs exactly where you need them using the custom spawn egg. Create instant crowds for your builds, markets, or bustling train stations.
